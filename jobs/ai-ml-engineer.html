AI / ML Engineer

Remote Full-time Engineering

About the Role

You'll build the core intelligence behind Bot Shift's AI operators. Our agents don't just answer questions — they run real business operations, make decisions, and drive revenue. You'll own the models, the orchestration, and the feedback loops that make them smarter over time.

What You'll Do

  • Design and build multi-agent systems that operate autonomously in live business environments
  • Fine-tune and evaluate LLMs for domain-specific tasks in hospitality, operations, and sales
  • Build retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) pipelines and knowledge management systems
  • Develop evaluation frameworks to measure agent performance against real business KPIs
  • Architect prompt chains, tool-use patterns, and agent orchestration layers
  • Collaborate directly with customers to understand operational needs and translate them into AI capabilities

What We're Looking For

  • 3+ years of experience in machine learning or AI engineering
  • Hands-on experience with LLMs (fine-tuning, prompt engineering, evaluation)
  • Strong Python skills and experience with ML frameworks (PyTorch, HuggingFace, etc.)
  • Experience building production ML systems, not just research prototypes
  • Familiarity with vector databases, embedding models, and retrieval systems
  • Ability to work autonomously and make judgment calls in ambiguous situations

Nice to Have

  • Experience with multi-agent frameworks (LangGraph, CrewAI, AutoGen)
  • Background in reinforcement learning or RLHF
  • Prior work in hospitality, retail, or other physical-world industries
  • Experience with robotics or embodied AI systems
